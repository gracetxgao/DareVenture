<h1>DareVenture</h1>

<p><b>DareVenture</b> is a mobile app where users are given daily prompts for wellness activities. Users can maintain their streak and earn venture points by completing the daily activity, which can be used to redeem discounts towards products of partnering outdoor apparel and equipment companies. Not only will users enjoy discounted items while working towards a healthy lifestyle, but DareVenture will also be able to work with a wide market in the fitness and wellbeing industry.</p>
